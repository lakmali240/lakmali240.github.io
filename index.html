About

My research asks where a model's structure should come from when labels, compute, or time are scarce. Sometimes the answer is an expert model, sometimes it is feedback from the task itself, and sometimes the structure has to be discovered from the data.

I apply these ideas to gigapixel whole-slide images, skin lesion images, battery degradation, and Alzheimer's disease neuropathology. At UC Davis, I work on foundation models and agentic vision–language systems for digital neuropathology.

Before my Ph.D., I spent three years as an Applications Engineer at Synopsys. During my Ph.D., I interned twice as an Applied Scientist at Amazon, building generative AI and multimodal machine learning pipelines.

Research

Selected projects, with the result each one is known for.

Now · UC Davis

AI for digital neuropathology

Foundation models and agentic vision–language systems that analyze gigapixel whole-slide brain images at scale, across institutions, scanners, and stains.

Alzheimer's disease and related dementias
MICCAI 2026

Learning which classes are hard

Dynamic Focal Attention learns a per-class attention bias during training, so the model focuses on classes that are actually difficult rather than just rare. Tested on brain whole-slide images, BCSS, and CRAG.

Up to +15.2 Dice on difficult classes
IEEE ICIP 2025

Active learning without the cold start

Task-aligned active learning rebuilds its selection space from the segmentation encoder every round, so the first labeled samples are already informative.

98.7% of full accuracy with 38.4% of labels
In preparation

Alzheimer's disease trajectories

CONTINUUM models disease progression as continuous, subtype-specific trajectories using amortized variational inference on autopsy data.

5 subtypes, reproduced in 2 independent cohorts
Targeting CVPR 2027

Concept removal in diffusion models

Temporally adaptive spectral control for removing unwanted concepts from diffusion models, acting only at the denoising steps that matter.

Generative AI safety
J. Manufacturing Systems 2024

Fast inference for expert models

A conditional invertible neural network, trained on simulated data, replaces MCMC for estimating battery degradation parameters.

690 s → 0.2 s per trajectory
